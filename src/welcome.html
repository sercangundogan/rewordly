Welcome to Rewordly

Your writing assistant for clearer, sharper text on any page.

What it does

Rewordly underlines spelling and grammar mistakes while you type, on almost any website and in any language, and rewrites selected text with AI when a sentence needs more than a fix.

Highlights

  • Checks as you type: Mistakes get a red underline. Hover it to see the fix and apply it in one click.
  • Any language: Each sentence is checked in its own language, automatically.
  • Smart rewrite: Adjust clarity, tone, and style without leaving the tab.
  • Context menu: Right-click selected text for Rewrite or Check grammar.
  • Works where you write: Gmail, X, Reddit, Medium, Notion, and more.

How to use

  1. Just write. When you finish a sentence, mistakes are underlined; hover to fix.
  2. Select text—the hover control appears; open it to get an improved version.
  3. Or right-click the selection and choose Rewrite or Check grammar.
  4. Use the extension popup for live checking, hover toggles, default tone, and account.
