What is Rewordly?

Rewordly helps you rewrite and improve your text with AI. Simply select any text on any website, and get instant suggestions to make your writing clearer, more professional, and error-free.

Key Features

  • Smart Rewriting: Improve clarity, tone, and style of your text with AI
  • Context Menu Access: Right-click on selected text to rewrite or check grammar
  • Site Optimized: Works perfectly on Gmail, Twitter/X, Reddit, Medium, Notion, and more

How to Use

  1. Select any text on a page, the rewrite button appears; click it to get an AI-improved version.
  2. Or right-click on selected text and choose "Rewrite" or "Check grammar" from the context menu.
  3. Use the extension popup to turn rewrite on/off or disable Rewordly on specific pages.
